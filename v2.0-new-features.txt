Traveller World & System Generator — What's New in v2.0
=========================================================

SECONDARY WORLD LAW LEVEL NOW FOLLOWS THE WBH THREE-CASE PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The law level rolled for secondary worlds (planets and moons that are not the
mainworld) now uses the full three-case procedure from World Builder's Handbook
pp.171-172, instead of the simplified captive-table-or-flat-roll approach used
previously.

- Captive governments (Government Code 6, not independently governed): the law
  level is determined by rolling 1D and comparing it to the mainworld's own law
  level. A low roll produces an independently-determined law level, a middling
  roll matches or is one above the mainworld, and a high roll adds a further
  1D on top. Penal Colony and Military Base designations shift the 1D roll
  upward, making extreme outcomes more likely.
- Low-authority governments (Codes 1-3, not independently governed): roll 2D
  and subtract the mainworld's government code. On a low result the secondary
  simply adopts the mainworld's law level; on a higher result a second 1D
  determines whether the secondary sets its own level or re-rolls independently.
- All other cases (independently governed worlds, or government codes outside
  the tables above): roll 2D-7 plus the government code. Freeport worlds
  (trading hubs with minimal government) get a -1 adjustment.

The "Independent Secondary World Government" checkbox (previously labelled
"Independent Government" in the desktop app, and "Indep Gov" in the web
interface) now clearly applies Case 3 across all government codes rather than
only affecting captive governments.

GEN-UI — RENAME STARS, WORLDS, AND MOONS AFTER GENERATING
-----------------------------------------------------------
Body names are no longer locked in once you generate a system. You can now
edit any name directly in the desktop app without regenerating.

- The world or system name shown in the result header is now a text field.
  Click it, type the new name, and press Enter — the system card and
  world card both update immediately, with no need to regenerate.
- An "Edit Names…" button opens a scrollable dialog listing every body in
  the system: stars, worlds (including the mainworld and asteroid belts),
  and moons. Each row shows a brief label — star designation and class,
  orbit position and world type, or which world a moon belongs to — next
  to a text field you can edit. Click OK to apply all changes at once, or
  Cancel to discard them.

WEB APP — RENAME STARS, WORLDS, AND MOONS AFTER GENERATING
-----------------------------------------------------------
The FastAPI web app now has the same name-editing capability as the desktop app.
After generating a full system, an "Edit Names…" button appears in the tab bar.
Clicking it opens a dialog listing every nameable body — stars, worlds, and moons —
each with a label and a text field. Edit any names you like, click OK, and both the
System tab and the Mainworld tab update to reflect the new names instantly.

GEN-UI — HORIZONTAL SCROLL BAR IN RESULT PANEL
-----------------------------------------------
The desktop app's result panel now scrolls horizontally when the window is narrowed.
The controls panel (Name, Seed, Generate, Options, source radio) stays fixed and
always visible. Only the result content — the system card, world card, or onboarding
placeholder — scrolls.
